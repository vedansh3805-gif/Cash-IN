SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

System Design & Decoupled Architecture (ADR 1 & 2)

1. Dual Presentation Tier

  • Responsive Web App: 10 modules + Component 3 login overlay.
  • EnterpriseChart: Custom Canvas 2D engine (<30KB), zero CDN, 100% offline, custom DPI scaling.
  • JavaFX 21 Companion: Native desktop GUI with 12 distinct views and 100vh compact monitor.
  • 5 Themes: Arctic Ledger, Forest, Copper, Ocean, Paper & Ink with instant canvas redraw.

2. Decoupled Boundary (ADR 2)

  • CashFlowUiService: Shared interface defining contracts for views.
  • BackendUiService: Connects views to Spring Boot 3 & SQLite.
  • MockUiService: Zero-DB in-memory mock engine; launches in <2s.
  • Zero View Coupling: Views have zero import of SQL, JDBC, or network drivers.

3. Engines & Persistence

  • ForecastService: Computes daily cash curve over 7/30/60/90 days against safety floor.
  • AnomalyService: Statistical Z-Score (>2.5) & 3x spend spikes.
  • CommunicationService: Dispatches Phone/SMS/Email notices with double-entry audit logging.
  • SQLite 3.45 ACID: Embedded single-file DB (cashflow.db) via HikariCP connection pool.

CORE INNOVATION

Specialized Capability: Multi-Channel Debtor Outreach (ADR 2, 5 & 6)

Dual-Party Notification & Audit Architecture:

Whenever a payment reminder is dispatched, CashFlow simultaneously alerts both the Debtor (urgent collection notice) and the Shopkeeper (instant verification copy). Every dispatch creates an immutable audit record in SQLite (communication_log table).

1. Native Dialer (tel:)

• Launches OS telephone dialer directly to buyer's phone.
• Modal shows overdue amount, late days, contact profile.
• Logs call outcome notes & promised payment date.

2. Dual-Party SMS

• Generates formatted SMS with invoice ID, due date, amount, bank details.
• Real-time 160-char counter.
• Sends SMS to debtor & auto-dispatches audit copy to Shopkeeper.

3. Statement Email & Bulk

• Compiles itemized billing statement with late fees and remittance terms.
• CCs shopkeeper automatically.
• 1-Click bulk alert notifies all delinquent buyers in one click.
